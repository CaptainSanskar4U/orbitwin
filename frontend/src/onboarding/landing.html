Mission workflow

One failure.
Seven steps.

From signal to solution. The workflow behind every recovery.

  1. 01

    Orbit

    The satellite runs normally, through sunlight and eclipse.

    All systems green.
  2. 02

    Strike

    An operator injects a battery fault.

    One command. Big impact.
  3. 03

    Detect

    The detector notices the early drift.

    Small signals. Big meaning.
  4. 04

    Explain

    The root-cause engine ranks the likely causes.

    Data turns into clarity.
  5. 05

    Advise

    The recovery advisor proposes safe actions.

    The right move. Always.
  6. 06

    Act

    The operator applies one, and the twin begins to recover.

    Autonomy in action.
  7. 07

    Record

    Every step is logged, with facts and recommendations kept apart.

    Learn. Improve. Repeat.
